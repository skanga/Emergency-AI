# CDC — Emergency drinking-water safety

## Boiling cloudy drinking water: chemical contamination and elevation limits

Follow local public-health advice about the water supply. Use safe bottled water if available. Water contaminated with fuel, toxic chemicals or radioactive material cannot be made safe by boiling or disinfecting it; obtain a different safe supply.

When the concern is germs and safe bottled water is unavailable, first pass cloudy water through a clean cloth, paper towel or coffee filter, or allow it to settle and draw off the clear water. Bring the clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute. Above 6,500 feet elevation, boil for 3 minutes. Let it cool and store it in clean, sanitized containers with tight covers.

This boiling summary does not provide a bleach dose; product ingredients and concentration must be known before using a product-specific disinfection procedure.
