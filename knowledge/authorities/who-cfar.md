# WHO — Emergency handover information

## SBAR handover: information for ambulance and receiving care

When handing a person over to emergency responders, organize the information clearly:

- Situation: identify yourself, your role and location, the person, the main problem and why further care is needed.
- Background: describe what happened, when and where it began, and known medical history, medicines and allergies.
- Assessment: report observations and care already provided, including any medicines taken or given. Do not invent missing findings.
- Recommendation: explain the help needed and any safety concerns. Ask the receiver to repeat back key information and clarify questions.

This communication summary does not authorize clinical assessment or treatment beyond your training.
