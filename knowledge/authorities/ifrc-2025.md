# IFRC — Scene safety before first aid

## Unsafe accident scene: hazards and getting help

Before approaching an accident or starting first aid, check for hazards to yourself, the injured person and bystanders. Look for a safe place from which to help. Do not enter an unsafe scene. If danger cannot be controlled, stay away and request the appropriate emergency service; support the person from a safe distance when possible.

Call for help as soon as you think it is needed. If you can safely provide care while calling, use speakerphone. Ask another person to help when available. Keep watching for changing hazards. Explain what you are doing and respect the person's privacy.
