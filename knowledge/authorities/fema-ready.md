# FEMA — Evacuation and shelter planning

## Evacuation or shelter: accessible transport and local instructions

Whether to evacuate or shelter depends on the hazard and whether the shelter provides protection. Follow current instructions from local authorities and emergency responders; a general guide cannot determine whether your particular building is safe.

Plan accessible meeting places in different directions, including one nearby and one farther away. Make sure household members know where to go and how to get there. Plan transport with family, friends or local government if you do not have a vehicle, and consider alternate routes and transport options.

When evacuating, leave early enough to avoid severe weather, take emergency supplies and follow recommended routes. Avoid shortcuts that may be blocked. Watch for damaged roads, bridges and downed power lines. Do not drive into flooded areas.
