# WHO — Snakebite first aid and transport

## Snakebite: keep still and arrange transport; do not walk to care

For a suspected snakebite, first get away from the immediate danger. Reassure the person, remove tight items around the bitten area before swelling, and then keep the person and bitten limb still. Arrange urgent transport to a health facility. Have helpers carry the person on a stretcher to transport rather than making the person walk; walking can accelerate venom effects.

Do not use a tight arterial tourniquet or unproven traditional remedies. Pressure-immobilization bandaging is not a universal treatment: WHO limits it to neurotoxic snakebites without local swelling. Do not infer that an unidentified snake qualifies. Monitor breathing while obtaining help. This is first aid pending urgent care, not home treatment of envenoming.
