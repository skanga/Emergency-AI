# FoodSafety.gov — Refrigerated food after a power outage

## Refrigerated soft cheese and milk: outage duration versus food temperature

Keep the refrigerator door closed as much as possible during a power outage. Refrigerated perishable food such as meat, poultry, fish, eggs and leftovers should be discarded after 4 hours without power. Never taste food to decide whether it is safe.

The chart uses a separate measured-temperature condition: food exposed to 40°F (4°C) or above for more than 2 hours. Under that condition, discard soft cheeses, shredded cheeses, low-fat cheeses, milk, cream and yogurt. Hard cheese such as Cheddar, Swiss and Parmesan may be kept under that chart condition. The hard-cheese exception does not apply to soft or shredded cheese.

These are selected refrigerated-food rows. They do not authorize keeping food contaminated by floodwater or establish a decision for every frozen food. Consult the complete chart for other foods; if safety is uncertain, discard the food.
