# Checklists: 72-hour kit and 1-year stockpile

Use these lists to prepare **before** and to take inventory **during** a crisis. Adjust to your family size, climate and special needs.

## 72-hour backpack (per person)

- [ ] Water: 9 liters (3 L/day) or a bottle + filter + chlorine tablets
- [ ] Ready-to-eat food for 3 days (bars, nuts, easy-open canned food, dried fruit)
- [ ] First aid kit + continuous-use medicines (7 days)
- [ ] Headlamp + spare batteries
- [ ] Battery/crank radio
- [ ] Lighter, matches in a sealed jar, ferro rod
- [ ] Multi-tool pocketknife
- [ ] Emergency blanket, rain poncho, change of clothes, socks, beanie
- [ ] Whistle, signal mirror
- [ ] PFF2/N95 masks
- [ ] Hygiene: soap, hand sanitizer gel, toilet paper, toothbrush and toothpaste, pads
- [ ] Documents in a plastic bag, cash in small bills, contact list on paper
- [ ] Portable charger (power bank) and cables
- [ ] USB flash drive with this kit (Emergency AI + Kiwix databases)
- [ ] Printed map of the region and compass
- [ ] Items for babies, elderly people and pets

## Home: 1-year stockpile (per adult, approximate)

### Water
- [ ] Storage of 50–100 L per person + rainwater harvesting system
- [ ] Ceramic/membrane filter with replacement candles/elements
- [ ] Unscented household bleach (replace every 6 months) or granular calcium hypochlorite (lasts longer)

### Food
- [ ] 150–180 kg of grains (rice, wheat, corn, oats, flours)
- [ ] 30–40 kg of legumes
- [ ] 10–12 L of oil/fat
- [ ] 25–30 kg of sugar/honey
- [ ] 3–5 kg of salt (more for preserving)
- [ ] Powdered milk, yeast, baking soda, vinegar, spices
- [ ] Multivitamin (365 doses)
- [ ] Hand grain mill
- [ ] Heirloom seeds for vegetables, beans, corn, squash

### Health
- [ ] Complete first aid kit (see [First aid](07-first-aid-medicine.md))
- [ ] Continuous-use medicines for as many months as the prescription allows
- [ ] Oral rehydration salts (several sachets)
- [ ] Spare glasses, hearing aids and batteries
- [ ] Printed books: *Onde Não Há Médico* (*Where There Is No Doctor*), *Onde Não Há Dentista* (*Where There Is No Dentist*)

### Fire, energy and light
- [ ] Lighters (10+), matches (several boxes), 2 ferro rods
- [ ] Dry firewood and/or charcoal; camp stove and fuel
- [ ] Solar panel + controller + battery
- [ ] LED flashlights, rechargeable batteries + solar charger
- [ ] Candles and lamp oil

### Hygiene
- [ ] Bar soap and laundry powder, dish detergent
- [ ] Toilet paper, heavy-duty garbage bags, quicklime/hydrated lime
- [ ] Buckets with lids (emergency latrine)
- [ ] Insect repellent, sunscreen, mosquito nets

### Tools and materials
- [ ] See [Tools](12-tools-knots-repairs.md): knife, axe, handsaw, shovel, hoe, hammer, nails, ropes, tarp, duct tape, wire
- [ ] Sewing kit
- [ ] Bicycle repair kit

### Communication and information
- [ ] AM/FM/shortwave radio
- [ ] Two-way radios (CB, PMR/FRS or amateur radio)
- [ ] Laptop or mini PC with this kit and the Kiwix databases; copy on an external hard drive
- [ ] Printed guides of the critical chapters of this repository

## Maintenance routine

- **Every 6 months:** replace stored water, check expiry dates of medicines and food, test flashlights and radios, recharge batteries.
- **Every year:** review the list as your family changes; practice: lighting fire, purifying water, CPR, building a shelter, using the radio.
